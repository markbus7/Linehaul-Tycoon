How to play

The goal

Build a freight network across the world. You do not move loads one at a time — you design lines, and the shape of your network decides whether you make money.

Lines — the whole game

A line is an ordered loop of cities. Vehicles you assign to it run that loop forever, picking up any freight waiting at a stop that is heading further round the loop, and dropping it when they reach it.

The order of the stops is the game. On a loop A→B→C→D, a load from A to D rides three legs. Order it A→D→C→B and the same load rides one. You pay fuel for every leg, on every cycle, forever — so a careless loop bleeds money quietly.

Reading a line

Each line shows its loop distance, cycle time, how many vehicles run it, and how full they are. A quiet stop warning means that stop has never produced a load worth carrying — it is costing you a detour for nothing. Cut it, or add a city that feeds it.

Freight

Loads pile up at your hubs and wait. Each one shows which of your lines will collect it, or no line if nothing you have built goes that way. That tag is your to-do list: unserved freight is money sitting on a dock.

A vehicle can only take a load if its capacity ≥ the weight. Refrigerated 🧊 loads need a reefer. Ships need ports at both ends, trains need rail — if a vehicle cannot run a loop, the line editor says so and refuses it.

Urgent tenders 🎯

Now and then a tender names a city and shows you no pin. Tap where you think it is on the map. Land within ~120km and it pays triple; the further off, the smaller the premium — but a wrong guess still pays the normal rate, so there is never a reason not to try.

Early tenders stay near your network and favour capitals. As your company grows the horizon widens, until it is asking you for cities on the other side of the world.

Learning the map 🌍

Every hub sits in a real country, shown with its flag throughout. Opening a hub gives you a briefing — country, capital, land borders, and why that city matters in freight. Tap any hub you own in Network to read it again. Countries you operate in light up on the map.

Fuel & upkeep ⛽

Fuel is charged per leg on the weight actually aboard, so empty legs still cost you. Every vehicle also costs a little per second whether it moves or not. Long loops with light loads are how companies go broke.

Levels

Delivering earns XP. Leveling unlocks new vehicles, hubs and upgrades, plus a cash bonus. XP is in the bar under the top menu — tap it for full stats.

The map

Pinch or scroll to zoom, drag to pan. Tap a moving vehicle to select it and have the camera follow it around Europe. Use ⊙ to reset the view.
